About Us

A learning companion for Kenya’s junior school learners.

Who we are

Junior School Learning Hub is an independent educational platform offering CBC-aligned learning resources for learners in Grades 7–9. The Hub is founded by Mr. Israel, an education mentor committed to making learning support clearer and more accessible.

Our resources include subject notes, worked examples, revision activities and practice quizzes. Materials are organised to help learners review concepts, practise skills and prepare for school-based assessments at their own pace.

Our purpose

We aim to support curiosity, regular practice and confidence in learning. We create and organise resources with the Kenyan Competency Based Curriculum in mind, while encouraging learners to use them alongside guidance from their teachers, parents and guardians.

How to use the Hub

  • Choose a grade and learning area that matches your current studies.
  • Read the notes, attempt practice questions and review feedback.
  • Ask a teacher or trusted adult for help when a topic is unclear.
  • Parents and guardians are encouraged to guide younger learners online and when creating accounts.
Independent resource: Junior School Learning Hub is not an official government website and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Ministry of Education or the Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development (KICD).
