# Research and implementation references

Reviewed for V6 architecture, 10 November 2026. Check current official publications again before production deployment.

1. KICD CBC Materials — curriculum designs across Pre-Primary, Primary, Junior School and Senior School: https://kicd.ac.ke/cbc-materials/
2. KNEC Regulations for Kenya Junior School Education Assessment (2025), especially section 13.2 grading bands and sections 13.3–13.8 reporting: https://www.knec.ac.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/KJSEA-REGULATIONS.pdf
3. KNEC User Guide on Management and Administration of KPSEA and KJSEA (2025), including school-based assessment evidence and School Year Reports: https://knec.ac.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/KPSEA-KJSEA-USER-GUIDE-03-DECEMBER-2025.docx-1.pdf
4. ODPC Guidance Note for the Education Sector: https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/ODPC-Guidance-Note-for-the-Education-Sector.pdf
5. ODPC Guidance Note for Processing Children's Data (2025): https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/ODPC-%E2%80%93-Guidance-Note-for-Processing-Childrens-Data.pdf
6. KNEC current circulars and guidelines: https://knec.ac.ke/circulars-guidelines/index.html

Research interpretation:
- KICD is the source for curriculum design and learning-area structure.
- KNEC is the source for national assessment regulations and grading profiles.
- ODPC guidance informs privacy and child-data safeguards.
- Vendor feature lists can inform workflow benchmarking but are not regulatory requirements and are not independent quality audits.
- V6 does not claim official KNEC, NEMIS or TSC integration.
