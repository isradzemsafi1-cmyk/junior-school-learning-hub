## Why no live AdSense code is included yet

The owner does not yet have an AdSense account or publisher ID. Do not add a guessed publisher ID, sample ad code, placeholder `ads.txt` seller record, or enable Auto ads. Google requires the publisher's own code and reviews the site before ads can serve.

## Safe activation sequence

1. The owner creates an AdSense account at https://www.google.com/adsense/start/ and adds `juniorschoollearninghub.co.ke`.
2. Complete Google's identity, payments and address verification steps as requested. Use accurate legal/payee details.
3. Copy the exact site-connection snippet and publisher ID from the AdSense account. Never share passwords or verification codes.
4. Before publishing ad code, review the entire site and update the public Privacy Policy to accurately describe Google advertising cookies, identifiers, data use and third-party ad serving. Do not claim ads are running until they are.
5. Confirm the audience treatment for each area. The Hub serves Grades 7–9 and may be used by children under 13 as well as teenagers. Follow Google's child-directed / age-restricted treatment requirements for the relevant pages and ad requests; do not enable personalized advertising for activity known to be from under-13 users or child-directed content.
6. Add only Google's exact `ads.txt` line after the publisher ID is issued. Verify that `https://juniorschoollearninghub.co.ke/ads.txt` returns it.
7. Add the approved AdSense verification snippet to the shared page head or the pages Google specifies. Keep the initial deployment on a preview branch and test before merging.
8. Request Google's site review in AdSense. Ads cannot be relied on until Google approves the site and the account is fully activated.

## Placement principles for this learning platform

- Start with restrained placements on public informational pages (home, About, articles/resources landing pages) only after policy and audience review.
- Do not place ads inside lesson text, answer choices, quiz controls, game controls, payment/checkout or M-Pesa confirmation screens, sign-in/registration forms, or near buttons that could cause accidental clicks.
- Do not interrupt a learner's lesson or assessment with overlays, pop-ups or interstitial ads.
- Keep paid-resource access and tuition/payment flows independent of advertising.
- Avoid ads on pages where the content, audience or consent status has not been reviewed.
