About Junior School Learning Hub

Learn Today. Lead Tomorrow. Junior School Learning Hub is a Kenyan education-support platform serving learners in Grades 7, 8 and 9, while also providing practical resources and tools for teachers, schools and families.

Our purpose

We aim to make quality, engaging and accessible learning support easier to reach. We bring learning materials, practice opportunities, teaching resources and school-support tools together in one digital hub.

What the Hub offers

  • Learner Centre: CBC-aligned learning notes, revision resources, quizzes and assessments for Grades 7–9.
  • Teacher Centre: Teaching and assessment support resources, including schemes of work, lesson plans, notes, topical questions and examinations.
  • Digital School Centre: Digital tools intended to support school academic and administrative work.
  • Career & Excellence: Career awareness, guidance and learner development resources.
  • Online Tuition: Information and access to online and physical tuition support.

Who we serve

Learners, teachers, parents, schools and education communities seeking practical support for junior school learning and school operations.

Our approach

We value clear explanations, purposeful practice, responsible use of technology, continuous improvement and respect for every learner’s potential.

Our motto: Learn Today. Lead Tomorrow.
