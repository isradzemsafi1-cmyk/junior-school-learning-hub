Your trust and privacy matter.

We explain what information may be handled when you visit or use our learning and school-support services, and why it is needed.

Learn how Junior School Learning Hub handles learner account details, learning activity, payment references, support messages and technical information.

🔒 Respectful handling🧒 Learner-aware approach🛡️ Reasonable safeguards

Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. Information we may collect

  • Account details: learner name, grade and sign-in information supplied during registration.
  • Learning activity: quiz attempts, scores, progress and resource-access records where these features are used.
  • Payment records: transaction references, payment status, amounts and related records needed to confirm purchases. We do not ask for your M-Pesa PIN.
  • Messages: information you choose to send by email or WhatsApp.
  • Technical information: device, browser, security and usage information processed by our website or service providers.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to create and manage accounts, provide learning resources, record quiz activity, confirm payments, respond to support requests, protect the website, maintain services and improve the learning experience.

3. Service providers

Some information may be processed by providers that help operate the platform, such as website hosting, database and authentication services, and payment providers including Safaricom M-Pesa where enabled. These providers may process information under their own terms and applicable arrangements.

4. Sharing information

We do not sell personal information. Limited information may be shared with service providers needed to operate the platform, where required by law, to protect users and the service, or with a parent, guardian or school where appropriate and lawful.

5. Children and learner information

Our services are intended to support junior school learners, including children. Parents and guardians should guide younger learners' use of online services and contact us if they have concerns about a learner's information.

6. Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards intended to protect information. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure. Information is retained for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain records, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with your account or request correction or deletion, subject to identity checks, technical limitations and legal or operational retention requirements.

8. Cookies, analytics and similar technologies

Essential browser storage or similar technologies may support sign-in, security and website functionality. If analytics or optional tracking tools are activated, this policy should be updated to identify those tools and explain their use accurately.

9. External links

Our website may link to third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.
