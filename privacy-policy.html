Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you use our learning services.

Effective date: 4 November 2026

This policy applies to the website at juniorschoollearninghub.co.ke and related online learning services operated as Junior School Learning Hub (“we”, “us” or “the Hub”). It is written to explain what the site’s current code and features may collect. We may update this policy if the services change.

Information we collect

  • When a learner account is created: the form requests a learner’s name, grade and email address, and Supabase authentication processes the account credentials. Passwords are submitted to the authentication provider and should not be shared with us by message.
  • When a quiz is saved to an account: the site can save grade, subject, score, percentage, answers and attempt time to the learner’s account/dashboard.
  • When registration is submitted: the form requests a learner’s name, parent/guardian phone number, grade, programme and any optional message. The current form opens WhatsApp with these details prefilled; it does not submit them to a Hub-operated registration database. They are shared only if the user chooses to send the WhatsApp message.
  • When making a payment: the site requests a phone number for the M-Pesa payment prompt and sends payment request details to its Netlify function. Safaricom processes the payment. Do not provide your M-Pesa PIN to the Hub.
  • Technical information: the website host, browser, or service providers may process standard connection and security information (for example, request details and IP address) to deliver and protect the site.

Why information is used

Information is used to provide learner accounts, show and save learning progress, respond to contact requests, process requested payments, maintain website security and operate the learning resources. We do not ask learners to provide sensitive personal information to use public learning pages.

Cookies and browser storage

In the homepage code inspected for this policy, we found no analytics, advertising or general tracking-cookie script. The Supabase client is used for authentication and may store session or authentication information in browser storage so sign-in can work. Your browser and hosting infrastructure may also use essential technical storage or logs. Google Fonts are requested from Google when pages load; Google may receive normal connection information as a result. Browser settings can control or clear browser storage, but clearing authentication storage may sign you out.

Analytics and advertising

The inspected site code does not currently include Google Analytics, another analytics tag, or an advertising network/AdSense script. No advertising cookies or personalised advertising integration are currently declared by the inspected code. If analytics or third-party advertising is added later, this policy should be updated to identify the relevant providers, data use, cookie/storage practices and any consent choices before those tools are enabled.

Service providers and sharing

Information may be processed by providers used to deliver requested services: Supabase for authentication and learner records; Netlify for hosting and serverless payment requests; Safaricom for M-Pesa payments; WhatsApp/Meta if a person chooses to send a prefilled message; and Google when the site requests Google Fonts. These providers handle data under their own terms and privacy notices. We do not sell learner personal information.

Children and teenagers

Our materials serve Grades 7–9 learners, including children and teenagers. Public learning pages can be viewed without an account. We aim to collect only information needed for an account or a requested service. A parent or guardian should help younger learners create accounts, review what is submitted, and contact us about a child’s information. Learners should not post or send addresses, school details, passwords, private photos, payment credentials or other sensitive information. We do not knowingly use the inspected site code to profile children for advertising or to serve targeted ads to them. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided information unnecessarily, contact us so we can review the request.

Retention and security

Account, quiz and transaction records may remain with the relevant service providers while needed to provide the service, meet operational or legal obligations, or resolve disputes. The current site does not publish a fixed deletion schedule. We use third-party providers for hosting, authentication and payments; no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to create an account or submit optional registration information. A parent/guardian or account holder may ask about access, correction or deletion of information by contacting us. We will take reasonable steps to assess and respond, subject to applicable law and information held by third-party providers. You may also manage browser storage using your device settings.

Contact about privacy

Contact us by calling or WhatsApp 0741 274 957. For child-related requests, please have a parent or guardian contact us. Do not include passwords or M-Pesa PINs.

Scope note: This policy reflects the repository code inspected for the requested update. Hosting-provider logs and third-party processing may occur outside the site code itself; check provider notices for their specific practices.
