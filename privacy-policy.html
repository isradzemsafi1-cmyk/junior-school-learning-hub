Privacy Policy

How Junior School Learning Hub handles information when you use the website and its learning and school-support services.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Junior School Learning Hub may collect, use, store and protect information when you visit or use our website.

1. Information we may collect

  • Account details: information such as a learner’s name, grade and sign-in details when an account is registered.
  • Learning activity: quiz attempts, scores, progress and resource-access records where these features are used.
  • Payment information: transaction references, payment status, amount and related records needed to confirm purchases. Payment credentials such as an M-Pesa PIN should never be shared with us.
  • Messages: information you choose to provide when contacting us by email or WhatsApp.
  • Technical information: basic device, browser, security and usage information that may be processed by our hosting or service providers.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and manage accounts, provide learning resources, record quiz activity, confirm payments, provide customer support, protect the website, maintain services and improve the learning experience.

3. Service providers

Some information may be processed by providers that help us operate the website, such as website hosting, database and authentication services, and payment providers including Safaricom M-Pesa where enabled. These providers process information under their own terms and applicable arrangements.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers needed to operate the platform, where required by law, to protect users and the service, or with a user’s parent, guardian or school where appropriate and lawful.

5. Children and learner information

Our services are intended to support junior school learners, including children. Parents and guardians should supervise younger learners’ use of online services and contact us if they have concerns about a learner’s information.

6. Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards intended to protect information. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain records, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with your account or to request correction or deletion, subject to identity checks, technical limitations and any legal or operational retention requirements.

8. Cookies and analytics

The website or its service providers may use essential browser storage or similar technologies to support sign-in, security and website functionality. If analytics or other optional tracking tools are enabled, this policy should be reviewed and updated to describe them accurately.

9. External links

Our website may link to third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.
