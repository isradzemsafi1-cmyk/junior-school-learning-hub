The CBC career journey

How career choices develop

Career interests grow through learning, practice and experience. Each stage helps learners recognise their strengths and make informed choices.

1

Pre-Primary · PP1–PP2

Explore through play, stories, movement, counting, creativity and interaction. Adults notice what learners enjoy.

2

Lower Primary · Grade 1–3

Try a variety of activities in language, mathematics, environment, creative work and social learning.

3

Upper Primary · Grade 4–6

Build confidence and recognise developing interests through learning areas, projects, clubs and activities.

4

Junior School · Grade 7–9

Explore broadly, reflect on strengths and aspirations, and prepare for pathway selection at the end of Grade 9.

Senior School · Grade 10–12
Learners pursue a chosen pathway and relevant tracks and subjects, continuing to build towards further education, training and careers.

Know yourself

Six areas to reflect on

A thoughtful career conversation looks beyond marks. Consider different parts of a learner’s development and the opportunities available to them.

🌟

Ability and interests

What a learner can do and enjoys doing, including activities that sustain motivation and engagement.

🧩

Aptitude

Areas where a learner may develop proficiency through learning, practice and experience.

🤝

Personality

Personal characteristics and ways of interacting, responding and working with others.

🎯

Career aspirations

Dreams, values and ambitions that can guide education, training and career exploration.

🏅

Co-curricular activities

Sports, music, drama, clubs, leadership and community service that reveal interests and competencies.

🌱

Opportunities

Mentorship, training, competitions, practical experiences and other chances to develop abilities.
