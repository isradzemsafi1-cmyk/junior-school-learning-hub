Term and curriculum guidance

The strand and sub-strand coverage follows the revised KICD Grade 7 Mathematics and Integrated Science curriculum designs. Your uploaded Karunga scheme specifically confirms the Mathematics Term I sequence under Numbers, including whole numbers, factors, fractions, decimals and squares/square roots.

The KICD curriculum designs list all Grade 7 strands but do not prescribe one universal term-by-term school sequence. For navigation, this resource groups Mathematics Numbers into Term 1; Algebra and Measurements into Term 2; and the remaining Measurements, Geometry and Data Handling into Term 3. Integrated Science is grouped as Scientific Investigation in Term 1; Mixtures, Elements and Compounds in Term 2; and Living Things and their Environment plus Force and Energy in Term 3. Treat this as a suggested distribution and align it with Karunga's approved schemes of work.

Important: Assessment questions are practice questions written for this learning resource, not official KNEC examination questions.
